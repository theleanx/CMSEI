How exposed is the supply of a material you buy?

CMSEI scores supply concentration for 19 critical minerals, from public USGS and IEA data.

Type a material in plain words. You get a market-level score from 0 to 100, the reasoning, the sources, and a few next steps. It is the same score for every user. It does not need your company's data.

What happens to what you type: your text is used once to find the material and is not saved or logged. Region and volume only choose which next-step suggestions you see. They never change the score and are not stored.
