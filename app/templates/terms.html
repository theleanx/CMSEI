Disclaimer

CMSEI (Critical Material Supply Exposure Index) is an informational, market-level screening view built from public data. It does not describe any company, supplier or individual, and it is not sourcing, legal, financial or investment advice. Scores are estimates that depend on published data of varying age and quality, and on modeling choices described on the Methodology page. Do not rely on a score alone to make a purchasing or compliance decision.

How we word things

  • We say "supply concentration exposure for the US market". A country is named only as a source of data, with a citation. We never call a country a threat.
  • We never name companies or suppliers.
  • Tier names are neutral and defined by numbers.

Corrections

Acknowledged within 5 business days. Decided within 15 business days. Accepted corrections change the data and appear in the public log with the date. Declined corrections state the reason. A government, company or trade body named in a source may submit a written response, which is linked from the material page.

Data

USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries (public domain). IEA Critical Minerals Market Review 2023 (CC BY 4.0, attribution required). Attribution is shown on each material page.

Export control

This site discusses publicly reported export-control subject matter in aggregate form only. It does not transact controlled goods or technology. A one-time legal review is pending.
