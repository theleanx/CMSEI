Accessibility

Self-assessment, not a certification. The site is built toward WCAG 2.2 AA and Section 508. No external audit has been done yet.

  • Every task works without JavaScript. Pages are server-rendered with semantic headings, landmarks, labelled form fields and a skip link.
  • Tiers are shown as text as well as colour. Charts have text equivalents (a table of the same values).
  • Layout adapts down to phone width with no sideways page scroll. Text can be enlarged to 200%.
  • Colours follow your light or dark setting and keep contrast of at least 4.5:1 for text.

Found a barrier? Use Report a correction and say "Accessibility" in the item field.
