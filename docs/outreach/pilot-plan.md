# Pilot plan: 5 to 10 plants through MEP centers or an association

**Goal.** Learn whether manufacturers act on the output, and find what is wrong, before wide promotion.

**Before starting** (blocking): refresh the data to MCS 2026 and complete the double-entry check (docs/DATA-REFRESH.md); have the second reviewer (Priyanshi Joshi) do the double entry; counsel review of Terms, Privacy and the compliance rules; confirm the CMSEI name is clear to use.

**Weeks 1-2: recruit.** Offer the one-pager to 2-3 MEP centers (NIST MEP lists centers by state) and one trade association. Ask each for two plants with a defense, electronics, EV or aerospace link.

**Weeks 3-6: run.** Each plant uploads one real parts list (or a sanitized one) and works through the questionnaire with two suppliers. Collect: did the matching find the right parts, did anyone contact a supplier, did anything surprise them.

**Weeks 7-8: decide.** Count: plants that got a usable ranking, suppliers that answered, errors found in the data or rules. Publish the findings, including negative ones. Fix before widening.

**What would count as success.** At least half the plants say they would use it again, at least one supplier gave origin information that changed a decision, and no error in the data or rules was left unfixed.

**What would count as stopping.** Matching is too poor to use without hand-tagging every part, or the data errors found are numerous enough that trust cannot be rebuilt cheaply.
